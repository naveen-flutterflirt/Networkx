Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the ITLearn360 Social Media Manager application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects user information.

Information We Collect

The App may access social media account information such as page IDs, account IDs, and access tokens solely for the purpose of publishing content explicitly requested by the user.

How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

  • Publish posts to Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts
  • Schedule and manage social media content
  • Improve application reliability and performance

Data Protection

Access tokens and sensitive data are stored securely in encrypted form and are never shared with third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

User Control

Users can revoke access at any time by removing the app from their social media account settings.
